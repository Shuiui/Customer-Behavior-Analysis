**📌 Project Overview**

This project analyzes customer shopping behavior using transactional data from 3,900 customer purchases. The objective is to uncover patterns in customer spending, subscription adoption, product preferences, discount utilization, and purchasing frequency to support data-driven business decisions.

The project combines Python for data cleaning and preprocessing, PostgreSQL for business analysis, and Power BI for interactive visualization.

**🎯 Business Objectives**
Understand customer purchasing patterns.
Identify high-value customer segments.
Evaluate the effectiveness of subscriptions and discounts.
Analyze product performance across categories.
Discover revenue-driving demographics.
Generate actionable business recommendations.
